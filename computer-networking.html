Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior networking knowledge?

No. The course starts with networking foundations and gradually builds practical configuration skills.

Is the course practical?

Yes. You practice LAN setup, router configuration, cable crimping and diagnostic commands.

Will I learn subnetting?

Yes. IPv4 structure, classes, CIDR, VLSM and subnet calculation are part of the curriculum.
