Frequently asked questions.

Do I need networking knowledge before CCNA?

Basic knowledge helps, and the course begins with TCP/IP, OSI and Ethernet concepts.

Is CCNA training practical?

Yes. AIT provides practical training for IOS, routing, switching, VLANs and troubleshooting.

Will I learn OSPF and EIGRP?

Yes. The curriculum includes both EIGRP and OSPF modules, alongside IP routing and WAN.
