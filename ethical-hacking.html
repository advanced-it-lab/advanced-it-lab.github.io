Course overview

Learn security research with an ethical mindset.

Ethical hacking helps organisations identify weaknesses before criminals exploit them. This course develops security awareness, technical foundations and responsible testing concepts.

Every topic is approached for authorised assessment, stronger defense and safer digital systems.
